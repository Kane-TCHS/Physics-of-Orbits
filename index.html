Formulas

Gravitational Potential Energy

U = −GMmR

Kinetic Energy

K = ½mv²

Total Energy of an Orbit (circular)

E = U + K = −GMm2R

Changing Orbit Altitudes

Echange = E2 − E1

The meaning of “R”

R = radius of Earth + satellite altitude

R is measured from the center of the planet, in meters.

Worked example

Gravitational Potential Energy (U)

A shuttle with a mass of 103,000 kg is flying 370 km above Earth’s surface. How much gravitational potential energy does it have?
  1. Step 1 · List what you knowm = 103,000 kg (shuttle) · altitude = 370 km · MEarth = 5.98 × 1024 kg · radius of Earth = 6.378 × 106 m
  2. Step 2 · Find R in meters370 km × 1,000 = 3.70 × 105 m
    R = 6.378 × 106 m + 3.70 × 105 m = 6.748 × 106 m
  3. Step 3 · Calculate the top of the fraction (G·M·m)(6.67 × 10−11)(5.98 × 1024)(103,000) = 4.108 × 1019
  4. Step 4 · Divide by R, and keep the minus signU = −4.108 × 10196.748 × 106 = −6.09 × 1012 J
    The slide labels this answer “E,” but it’s the potential energy U.
Worked example

Changing Orbit Altitudes

A news station wants to move a 12,000 kg weather satellite, in a circular orbit, from 650 km above Earth’s surface to an orbit 800 km above Earth’s surface. How much energy will this procedure require?
  1. Step 1 · Find both R values in metersR1 = 6.378 × 106 + 6.50 × 105 = 7.028 × 106 m
    R2 = 6.378 × 106 + 8.00 × 105 = 7.178 × 106 m
  2. Step 2 · Total energy in the starting orbitE1 = −(6.67 × 10−11)(5.98 × 1024)(12,000)2(7.028 × 106) = −3.405 × 1011 J
  3. Step 3 · Total energy in the final orbitE2 = −(6.67 × 10−11)(5.98 × 1024)(12,000)2(7.178 × 106) = −3.334 × 1011 J
  4. Step 4 · Final minus initialEchange = E2 − E1 = (−3.334 × 1011) − (−3.405 × 1011) = +7.1 × 109 J
    The positive sign means energy must be added to change orbits.
  5. ⚠ Watch out · Don’t round too earlyThe slide shows +8 × 109 J. It got that by rounding E1 and E2 to −3.41 and −3.33 before subtracting. When two numbers are this close together, small rounding errors become a big error in the difference. Keep at least four digits until the final step.
